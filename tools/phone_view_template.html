Races come from the NAPA race matrix transcribed in src/race.py — the band is the stronger player's CSR for that game, and the race follows from the gap. It is a static league-wide table, not a live lookup.

Loaded is how many already-played fixtures have score sheets in the database, out of those whose date has passed. A gap is normally a pending makeup — a match scheduled but not yet played, which the catch-up queue keeps re-pulling until it lands.
